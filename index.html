OfferSend Privacy

Score the listings. Send the offers.

OfferSend reads your MLS export, ranks every listing by how motivated the seller looks, fills your purchase contract, and emails the offer to the listing agent from your own address.

Built for real estate investors and wholesalers who write a lot of offers and would rather spend the day on the ones worth writing.

How it works

  1. 1

    Import

    Export listings from your MLS and drop the file in. Columns map themselves.

  2. 2

    Score

    Every listing is ranked on price reductions, days on market, and the language in the remarks.

  3. 3

    Send

    Your contract is filled and emailed to the listing agent, from your address, with the PDF attached.

  4. 4

    Track

    Every offer is logged, so you know what went out, to whom, and what came back.

Sending from your own email

Why OfferSend asks to connect your Gmail

An offer should arrive from the person making it. When you connect your Google Account, OfferSend can send each approved offer from your own address, so the listing agent sees your name and can simply reply to you.

OfferSend requests one permission, send email on your behalf, and nothing more:

  • It cannot read, search or open any message in your mailbox.
  • It sends only the offers you have reviewed and approved, one at a time.
  • You can disconnect it from your Google Account at any time.
